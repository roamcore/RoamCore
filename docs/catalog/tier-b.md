# Support tier B

One long page listing every feature currently tagged with this tier, grouped by category.

Tip: use your browser’s find (Ctrl/Cmd+F) to jump quickly (e.g. ‘victron’, ‘tailscale’).

## Audio Media

- [Music Assistant](audio-media/music-assistant/) — Music Assistant is a powerful music system that can unify multiple speakers/targets and give you a consistent “play everywhere” experience.

## Homelab

- [AdGuard Home (network-wide ad blocking)](homelab/adguard-home/) — AdGuard Home is an alternative to Pi-hole: DNS-based ad/tracker blocking with a nice UI.
- [NAS (Network Attached Storage)](homelab/nas/) — A NAS gives you reliable local storage for media, camera footage, backups, and logs—especially useful when you don’t want to depend on cloud
- [Pi-hole (network-wide ad blocking)](homelab/pi-hole/) — Pi-hole blocks ads and trackers for every device on your network by acting as DNS.

## Safety

- [Deadbolts (smart locks)](safety/deadbolts/) — Smart deadbolts let you monitor lock state (locked/unlocked) and (optionally) control it from Home Assistant.
- [Smoke / CO / Gas sensors](safety/smoke-co-gas-sensors/) — Safety sensors that trigger clear alerts when something dangerous happens: smoke, carbon monoxide, propane/LPG, or other gases.
