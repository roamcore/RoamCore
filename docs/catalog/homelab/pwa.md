# RoamCore on your phone (installable app)

RoamCore can live on your phone's home screen like a regular app — one tap to open, works even when the internet is gone.

## What it does

RoamCore is an installable dashboard for your phone. It looks like a regular app icon, opens full-screen, and keeps the last screen you saw when the Hub is unreachable — so you always see something honest instead of a blank screen.
