## What RoamCore does NOT do

RoamCore is **not** a life-safety system. Smoke/CO/gas sensors in the
van should be standalone, battery-backed units (like the kidde or
first alert models) — not relying on any software. RoamCore's tiles
are a *convenience* layer on top of dedicated hardware.
