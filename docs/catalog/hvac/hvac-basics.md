# HVAC control (heating/cooling foundations)

**Support tier:** A (RoamCore native)

## What this is
RoamCore includes scaffolding and patterns for HVAC-related controls/tiles (temperature, comfort, alerts). Specific heater/AC hardware integrations vary.

## Why it’s useful in a van
- Comfort and safety in extreme temps
- Better power management (heat/cool only when needed)

## Extra hardware required
- Depends on your heater/AC hardware and integration

## Install / best next step
- Start by integrating your thermostat/heater into HA, then map entities into RoamCore tiles.
