# Fans

Fan control — roof vents, circulation fans, smart-fan integrations.

The full fan guide lives in the Connections section:

- **[Connection: Fans](../../connections/fans/docs/recipe.md)** — vendor-neutral fan-controller umbrella covering Z-Wave / Zigbee / MQTT fan controllers, Wi-Fi / BLE smart fans, generic 12 V / 24 V fans via relay, and all-in-one rooftop vent fans. Includes the `rc_fan_*` contract tiles and automations for humidity, temperature, rain, and sleep-mode suppression. **Community Verified** — tested on a real van by a member of the community.

See the connection page for install instructions, supported hardware, and
how to wire fans into your RoamCore dashboard.
