# Happijac bed lift

Bed lift control — van bed up / down.

## What you need

- Nothing extra — uses what's already in the van.

## Install

- Click **Add to my van** in the RoamCore dashboard, **or** run `bash <(curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/roamcore/RoamCore/main/install.sh) --feature happijac`.
- Restart Home Assistant.
- Done — the tiles appear under the relevant section in the dashboard.

## What it shows on your dashboard

- Lift position
- Lift up limit
- Lift down limit
- Lift moving
- Lift position pct
- Lift safety ok
- Lift lift
- Lift lower
- Lift stop
- Lift obstruction detected
- Lift low voltage lockout
- Lift mode
