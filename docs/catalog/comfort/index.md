# Comfort

- **[Approach lights](approach-lights.md)** — Approach lights (welcome-home exterior + underbody lighting) — the universal small-comfort van automation: open the door after dark, the underbody + entry + soft-interior lights come on for a configurable duration (default 2 min) so you can see where you're stepping and feel like the van is welcoming you home.
- **[Fans](fans.md)** — Fans (vendor-neutral fan-controller umbrella for HA, covering rooftop vent fans + circulation fans + bathroom exhaust fans — rooftop + circulation fans cover the climate-aware airflow + the rain-sensor safety block; bathroom exhaust fans wire as a separate downstream `fan.
- **[HVAC basics](hvac-basics.md)** — HVAC basics — cabin heating/cooling foundations for vans.
- **[Happijac bed lift](happijac.md)** — Bed lift control — van bed up / down.
- **[Heated floors + engine pre-heat](heated-floors.md)** — Heated floors + engine pre-heat — cold-weather comfort controls for vans — are the foundation of every "Warm up" automation in winter van life.
- **[Motion-based lighting](motion-based-lighting.md)** — Motion-based lighting (driving + arrival).
- **[Music Assistant](music-assistant.md)** — Music Assistant is a provider- agnostic multi-room audio orchestrator very popular in HA installs — it unifies Spotify, Apple Music, TuneIn/radio, local files, and Chromecast/AirPlay/Sonos receivers behind a single "play everywhere" surface with per-zone controls.
