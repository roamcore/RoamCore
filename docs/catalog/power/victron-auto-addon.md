# Victron Auto add-on (backend connector)

## What it does
A Home Assistant add-on used by RoamCore to connect to Victron telemetry automatically and keep power entities up to date.

## Why it’s useful in a van
- Less manual wiring
- More reliable power telemetry

## How to install
- Victron GX device

- Add-on: `homeassistant/addons/roamcore-victron-auto`
- Runbook: `docs/runbooks/victron-integration.md`
